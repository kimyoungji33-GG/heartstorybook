**2. The red marks he noticed first**

Ren drew a red line where the explanations in the later pages could be reordered. “What if you moved this explanation earlier?” Davi nodded. That would make the presentation easier to follow. He especially wanted to know whether the example he had worked so hard on for the first page was clear.

**3. Before finding the words**

Davi straightened the scattered pages and looked at the first one again. ‘Should I take this example out too?’ Ren had not told him to remove it, but hearing one suggested change after another had made Davi less sure of himself. **4. What did you think of the first example?**

“I'll change the order of the explanations. But what did you think of the example on the first page? Does it make clear how the booklet would be used?” Davi asked. Ren put down his pen and read the first page again. “Yes. **5. Let’s keep the first page**

“Let's keep the first page and just change the order of the explanations later on,” Ren said.
